# Tarven – Karryn-inspired guest types, preferences & systems
*(Hard lines remain: all intimate acts require explicit player choice. Pull away always works. Too-drunk blocks yes. Force bar is intensity feedback only.)*

## Guest factions with preferred body areas
Each faction has a base Nerve and a preferred desire meter. When they reach, the touch is weighted toward their preference (still gated by Nerve and the player’s matching Desire).

| Faction       | Base Nerve | Preferred meter | Flavour notes                                      | Typical boldness range |
|---------------|------------|-----------------|----------------------------------------------------|------------------------|
| Farmers       | 1          | Body / Mouth    | Gentle, lap, hand, hair, cheek                     | 1–5                    |
| Fugitives     | 1          | Neck / Body     | Cautious, secretive, hair, neck, waist             | 1–6                    |
| Smugglers     | 2          | Pussy           | Under-table, thigh, lap, intimate                  | 3–8                    |
| Soldiers      | 3          | Butt / Breasts  | Physical, rear, chest, pins, carries               | 4–9                    |
| Knights (sub) | 3          | Butt / Breasts  | Same as soldiers, more formal                      | 4–9                    |

- Preferred meter gets +30–50 % weight when picking a touch.
- Allowing a preferred-meter touch gives extra tip and a small extra rise to that meter.
- Force bar still shows the actual boldness of the chosen touch (1–10).

## Named regulars & “Wanted” system
- 1–2 named guests per faction can become permanent regulars if they take a “first” (first kiss, first intimate, first upstairs, etc.) or cause enough defeats in After Hours / brawls.
- They reappear later nights with rising Nerve and a fixed preferred meter.
- Each time you beat them physically (brawl) or win an After Hours duel they grow stronger next time (higher Nerve, more aggressive weighting).
- They remember previous nights and comment on it.
- Optional: a “Wanted” journal tab that lists them and their current strength / preference.

## Guest states
States can stack with mood and affect Nerve or touch choice:

| State     | Effect                                                                 |
|-----------|------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Horny     | +1.5 Nerve, strongly prefers their faction meter, higher Force visuals |
| Eager     | Extra tip if player Leans in; slightly higher chance to reach          |
| Shy       | –1 Nerve until player allows at least one touch                        |
| Erect     | (flavour) slight penalty to their “critical” resistance in After Hours |
| Drunk     | Existing rules: too-drunk blocks yes from either side                  |

Name prefixes (Great, Bold, Fat, Quiet, etc.) can still apply small stat buffs/debuffs as already planned.

## Force bar integration
- Force bar continues to show current touch boldness (1–10) + mind stage.
- When a guest is Horny or targeting their preferred meter, the bar can flash or gain a small icon.
- High Force (6+) still just means higher intensity; player choice is unchanged.

## Player side (already present, reinforced)
- Five desire meters (Body, Mouth, Breasts, Butt, Pussy) work exactly as designed.
- Pussy meter is the direct analogue of Karryn’s genital desire.
- Passives for each meter stay; preferred-guest interactions can count toward the relevant group faster.
- After Hours duels remain fully consensual and only use parts the player put in play.

## Optional expansions (still hard-line)
- Guest-type preferred “stances” in After Hours (soldiers prefer rear/riding, smugglers prefer hands/mouth under table flavour, etc.).
- Small starting leanings or earned traits that raise one meter’s starting value (player choice).
- Environment modifiers (private booths raise Pussy/Mouth chance, bar stage raises Body/Butt, etc.).

All of the above keep the existing consent pillars intact. Force is feedback. Choice is mandatory.
