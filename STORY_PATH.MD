
# Last Call at Tarven: Story

> The story design document. It replaces the story parts of GAME_CONCEPT.md (sections 1, 8.2, 9 and 11) once it is approved.
> **Draft 3.0, 14 Nov 2026.** Major update: personal build-up stages + Karryn-style integration.

**House rules stay in force.** Every character is an adult (18+), and the game says so. Consent toggle controls pressure:
- **Consent On**: Every intimate beat is her choice. Player is asked first. Slap and Pull away always work. Too drunk blocks every yes. Force bar is feedback only after a yes.
- **Consent Off**: High-Nerve / Horny guests may land touches without the choice screen. Brawl loss can push harder. Pull/Slap still available on the next reach. Force bar fills faster.
Sexual scenes stay as non-graphic 18+ cards.

> **The main goal is popularity.** She starts with one cold tavern and a debt, and aims to make it **the most popular house on the pass**. She beats the rival taverns, buys them up, and decides whether to work with the bosses she beat, the Baron included. The red book is the story underneath.

> **Fans drive the story, nights don't.** Story beats fire on fan count. Nights keep passing and bring small things, but no night count forces a beat.

> **Current scope: Chapter 1, Tarven.**

## 1. Why the old story flops

| Problem | What it looks like in the game now |
|---|---|
| **The story ignores most of the game** | Corruption, lewdness, willpower, passives, brawls, after-hours and the personal slide have little meaning in the story. |
| **Nobody has a name** | Guests are generic. Betraying someone with no face costs nothing. |
| **No visible personal arc** | She starts sheltered but the text does not show resistance, firsts, or staged change. |
| **Last call is flat** | Same numbers every night. Selling always pays best. |
| **Rumours don't connect** | Separate threads. What you did rarely comes back. |
| **No origin weight** | Why she barely knew Wren, why the family left, why the tavern goes to her. |

What already works and should stay: the tavern loop, the Baron's debt, Wren, last call, rumours, dawn report, desire meters, passives, and the consent toggle.

## 3. The spine

**On the surface:** climb from a cold, indebted tavern to the most popular house on the pass.

**Underneath:**
- The Baron wants the red book, not just the gold.
- The debt is forged. Wren was poisoned.
- Wren split the book because half the village is in it.

The heroine's real question is **"whose granddaughter am I?"**:
- Wren the madam
- Wren the keeper
- or someone new

**New:** Her personal slide (Lewdness stages) and her moral choices (Corruption) both answer that question. The top tavern still decides who hosts the auditor.

---

## 4. Who she becomes — Personal Stages (New)

Lewdness + key firsts define four stages. These change her inner thoughts, guest dialogue, morning reports, and some story reactions. Corruption stays independent.

| Stage | Trigger (approx.) | Inner feel | Guest reaction | Story echo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sheltered** | Lewdness 0–15, almost no allows | Awkward, surprised, “I’ve never…”, pulls away more in text | Polite or testing | Stronger link to Mother’s secrecy |
| **Curious** | 15–35, first kiss / chest / thigh | Torn thoughts common, some leaning | Bolder reaches, more rumours | First real temptation with named guests |
| **Willing** | 35–60, several upstairs or high meters | More confident, teases back sometimes | Regulars form, higher nerve | Can start offering (passives support this) |
| **Known** | 60+, multiple kinds upstairs | Open or conflicted depending on Corruption | People come because of her reputation | Affects Baron, rivals, village talk |

**Consent toggle effect on stages:**
- Consent On: thoughts stay choice-focused (“I let him…”).
- Consent Off: thoughts acknowledge pressure (“I didn’t stop him in time…”).

Four end-states remain:

| | Low Corruption | High Corruption |
|---|---|---|
| Low Lewdness | The Keeper | The Schemer |
| High Lewdness | The Fair Madam | The Rotten Madam |

## 6. How each system carries the story

| System | What it means in the story |
|---|---|
| **Lewdness + Stages** | Visible personal arc. Changes thoughts, dialogue, and how the town talks. |
| **Corruption** | Moral slide. Opens dark choices and changes ending flavour. |
| **Desire meters & Passives** | The actual engine of the build-up. Unlock thoughts at bedtime should feel like real moments. |
| **Consent toggle** | Consent On = deliberate slide. Consent Off = more Karryn-style pressure. |
| **Named regulars** | Make the slide personal. They remember and grow bolder. |
| **Fans / Popularity** | Still the main goal. High Lewdness can help or hurt depending on stage and Corruption. |
| **Red book / Wren** | Personal mirror. High stages make the question “whose granddaughter” sharper. |

## 7. Practical writing guidelines

### Inner thoughts (same touch, different stages)

**Hand on waist (boldness 3):**
- Sheltered: “(His hand is on my waist. I should move.)”
- Curious: “(It feels… warm. I don’t hate it.)”
- Willing: “(He knows where to put it now.)”
- Known + Consent Off: “(They just reach now. I let them.)”

**First intimate touch:**
- Always special. “My first time anyone’s touched me there. In the middle of the tavern.”

### Morning report additions
- Show current stage.
- “The village is talking about…” (changes with stage + recent allows).
- New passives listed with her thought.

### Early Act 1 beats to strengthen
1. First few nights: more resistance text even if the player allows.
2. First kiss with a named guest: full first-time weight.
3. After first upstairs: village talk shifts; some allies worry, rivals notice.
4. One Last Call choice feels different if she is already Willing or Known.

---

## 8. Endings note

Endings should feel like the natural result of the path:
- Fair Madam (high Lewdness, low Corruption): she hangs the lantern and protects people.
- Rotten Madam (high both): rich, feared, alone. Girls turn with her.
- Keeper / Schemer: low personal slide, different moral choices.
