## Overview

Most people who walk into the Church of Veyra for the first time are sexually frustrated and have never done anything like this. They come because their partner is cold, they have no partner, or they simply feel tight and unsatisfied. The church’s job is to take that ordinary frustration and, step by step, turn the person into someone who dresses, talks, and acts very slutty — both inside the temple and, eventually, outside it.

The recruitment is not a single night. It is a short arc that moves a new visitor from “I’ve never done this” to “I can’t stop thinking about it.”

## Recruitment Arc (3 Stages)

### Stage 1 — First Visit (Never Experienced)
The person arrives in normal clothes, nervous, often looking at the floor.

**What the priest says (example):**
> “You don’t have to take anything off yet. Just tell me where it hurts. Is it that your partner stopped touching you? Or that no one has touched you at all? Veyra doesn’t care which. She only cares that the tightness leaves.”

**What happens:**
- Private talking session only.
- Light touching over clothes if the visitor agrees.
- They leave still dressed normally, but with a clear invitation to return.
- They are given a simple token (a small ribbon or a drop of scented oil) so they remember.

**Goal of this stage:** Make them feel safe enough to come back.

### Stage 2 — Second and Third Visits (Starting to Open)
The visitor returns. They are still in ordinary clothes but less nervous.

**What the priest says:**
> “Last time you only talked. This time you can stay longer. Some people who come here start wearing less once they feel safe. You don’t have to. But if you want to feel what the others feel, we can start small.”

**What happens:**
- They are offered a lighter, more open outfit to change into (still not full slut gear).
- First real sex play (hands, mouth, or a small toy) while the priest talks them through it.
- They are encouraged to say what feels good out loud.
- Before they leave, a junior priest or deacon shows them the full slut outfits the higher ranks wear and says, “This is what it looks like when someone stops holding back.”

**Goal of this stage:** Make them associate the church with pleasure and start wanting to look and act the way the devotees do.

### Stage 3 — Commitment (Becoming Slutty)
The visitor now comes regularly. They ask about the outfits. They stay longer. They start initiating.

**What the priest says:**
> “You’ve been coming for a while and the tightness keeps returning the moment you leave. That means your body already belongs here more than it belongs out there. If you want to stop fighting it, we can put you in the clothes the others wear and keep a toy inside you so the feeling doesn’t fade when you walk out the door.”

**What happens:**
- They are given a full slut outfit (short, open, easy access).
- An internal toy is inserted and they are told to keep it in while they are in the temple.
- They are asked to walk through the common areas so other visitors and priests can see them.
- They are given simple lines to practice (“I need it,” “Please use me,” “I came back because I was still empty”).
- If they accept, they are moved onto the clergy track as an Acolyte or Deacon and the toy/outfit rules begin to apply.

**Goal of this stage:** Turn a normal frustrated townsperson into someone who dresses, talks, and behaves in a very slutty way inside the church — and who starts carrying that attitude back into town.

## Sample Dialogue Lines (Ready to Drop In)

**Priest to a first-timer:**
- “You look like you’ve been holding it in for a long time. You don’t have to hold it here.”
- “Tell me what your partner won’t do. Or tell me what you’ve never had anyone do. Either is fine.”
- “Most people who walk in that door have never done anything like this. That’s why they come.”

**Priest when the visitor starts to want more:**
- “You keep coming back in the same clothes. Do you want to try what the others wear?”
- “The ones who stay empty the longest are the ones who only visit. The ones who keep a toy in leave satisfied.”
- “Say it out loud. Say you want to be used. It makes the relief stronger.”

**Lines the new recruit is encouraged to say:**
- “I never did this before I came here.”
- “My partner doesn’t touch me like this.”
- “Please don’t stop. I was so tight.”
- “I want to wear what they wear.”
- “Leave it in. I don’t want to feel empty when I go home.”

## How It Spreads in Town

Once a few people have gone through the arc, the effect shows up in ordinary life:

- A wife who used to dress modestly starts wearing shorter skirts and lower necklines even outside the temple.
- A husband who was quiet now makes crude jokes and stares more openly.
- People who used to only complain about their sex life in the tavern now casually mention “going to the Warm House” and describe what happened in detail.
- The more recruits the church has, the more normal the slutty clothing and open talk become among a certain circle in Tarven.

The official chapel and the Baron notice. Some call it corruption. Others call it useful. The tavern hears all of it.

## Quick Reference for Writing

- New recruits start completely inexperienced with the cult.
- The church never forces the first visit, but once someone keeps returning it steadily pushes them toward slutty clothing, open speech, and internal toys.
- The end state for a successful recruitment is a townsperson who now looks, talks, and acts very slutty — at least when they are connected to the church.
- All of this can be turned into guest dialogue, bedtime scenes, or a side location the player can visit later.
