# Last Call at Tarven: Game Concept

> **Rating: Adults only (18+).** Sexual content, nudity, heavy drinking, crude language, gambling, violence and moral corruption. Every character in the game is an adult, and the game says so in writing.

---

## 1. Pitch

You inherit your late grandmother's tavern in **Tarven**, a market town on a mountain pass, along with her debt to **the Baron**. He comes to collect in a few nights. Until then you pour ale, cook stew, clean up after drunks, and listen. Farmers, soldiers, smugglers and fugitives all come through your door, and once they've had a few drinks they talk.

Every night you decide what kind of innkeeper you are. You can stay proper and respected, or become the flirt of the pass. You can protect your guests or sell their secrets to the tax collector. You can keep your clothes on, or let the room have a show for a better tip.

**In one line:** a pixel-art tavern sim where everything you serve, say, sell and show feeds a reputation the whole town gossips about, and that reputation decides whether you keep the tavern, lose it, end up in irons, or bring the Baron down.

---

## 2. Pillars

1. **Running the bar is the game.** Moving between stations, pouring, serving, cooking and washing up under time pressure. The adult content sits on top of this loop and never replaces it.
2. **Reputation is what you play with.** Flirt, Respect, Fame, Kindness, Greed and the others are your real resources. Guests react to who you've become.
3. **Desire has a price.** Lust and flirting bring in money, tips and favours, and they cost you respect, safety and how people talk about you. Every intimate choice also moves the economy and the story.
4. **Gossip is power.** Secrets are worth more than stew. Whatever you hear can be kept, traded or used against someone.
5. **Always the player's choice.** Nothing sexual happens to the player character without the player agreeing to it, and every scene can be turned down with no dead end.

---

## 3. Setting and tone

- **Tarven** is a cold little market town under the mountains. One high street, a chapel, a quarry, and the Baron's keep watching over it all.
- **The tavern** is the biggest building on the street. It's cramped and worn when you arrive, and you can rebuild it (see House Plans).
- **Tone:** earthy, bawdy and warm, with something darker underneath. Think of a tavern full of dirty songs where the Baron's men are always sitting at the back table. The humour is crude, the romance is sincere, and the politics are nasty.
- **Look:** low-res 3D rendered through a dithered 5-bit filter, pixel-art HUD, comic-style panels cut on the slant for big moments, and a visual-novel layer for story beats.

## 4. The player character

Pick one of **two siblings** (male or female body). Each is fully customisable:

- **Body:** height, build, bust (size, lift, spacing, projection), rear (size, lift, width, projection)
- **Face, hair (five styles each), skin**
- **Clothes:** layered garments, any of which can be removed. Removed garments can be taken in strip dares (see 7.3 and 7.4).

Your look is saved with the run. The NPCs' writing adapts to the body you picked ("handsome" or "beautiful", what they compliment, which garment they reach for).

## 5. Core loop

### 5.1 One day

| Phase | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Day** | Shop, clean, use House Plans to build and place furniture, visit the town (planned) |
| **Dusk** | Prepare: tap the cask, bake bread, start the stew |
| **Service** | Guests arrive in parties. Take orders, serve, clear tables, wash cups and dishes, mop spills. Talk, drink with guests, take dares |
| **Last Call** | A visitor (the tax collector, a captain, a stranger) asks what you heard tonight. Tell the truth, lie, sell it, or say nothing |
| **Bedtime** | The tavern is closed. Private moments happen here (see 7.4) |
| **Dawn** | Results: gold, tips, reputation changes, and what the town is now saying about you |

### 5.2 Service mechanics (already built)

- Stations: **tap, bread basket, stew pot, barrel, bin, wash tub**
- Menu: **ale, bread, stew, whole casks**
- Stew burns if you leave it. Cups and dishes run out if you don't wash them.
- Each guest has **patience**, a **mood**, a **tip** and a **stay length**.
- Mess builds up: dirty dishes, ale puddles, dust.
- Drinking with guests loosens their tongues and lowers your coordination (pours get slower).

### 5.3 Run structure

- **Current build:** 3 nights, a 150-gold debt.
- **Planned full game:** 3 acts across about 15 nights. The Baron raises the debt, the factions pull harder, and each act opens a new floor of the tavern and new kinds of guests.

## 6. Stats and reputation

### 6.1 Persona (how the town sees you)

| Stat | Range | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Flirt** | 0–10 | From proper to the shameless charmer of the pass |
| **Respect** | − to + | Drops when you flirt back, get sloppy drunk or sell people out. Rises with manners and protecting people |
| **Fame** | 0–100 | How far your name has travelled. High fame brings guests who idolise you |
| **Kindness / Greed** | | How you treat people who can't pay, and how hard you squeeze the ones who can |
| **Drinks** | | How often you drink on the job |
| **Protected / Informed / Sold** | | What you did with secrets |
| **Lust / Desires** | | The heat you stir up in the room, and what guests come in longing for |

### 6.2 Story meters

- **Gold.** Must cover the debt.
- **Trust.** The village's faith in you. High enough and they'll stand up to the Baron.
- **Heat.** The Baron's suspicion. At the maximum, soldiers break down your door.

### 6.3 Guest moods

Calm, Happy, Angry, **Lustful**, Idolizing, Drunk, Gloomy, Scared, Scheming.
Mood depends on the guest's type, your reputation and the room's mess, and it changes their patience, tips, how long they stay and whether they spill. A Lustful guest tips more and stays longer, and is more likely to dare, grope at your clothes or proposition you.

## 7. Adult content

The adult content grows out of the systems above, mainly Flirt, Respect, Lust and Gold. It isn't a separate mode.

### 7.1 Content rules (non-negotiable)

- **All characters are 18 or older.** Every romanceable or sexualised NPC has a stated adult age and an adult body and design. No childlike characters, bodies or writing, ever.
- **Consent.** Every sexual or nudity beat is a choice the player makes. Turning it down is always an option, it's always written as a real answer, and it never locks the player out of progress.
- **No sexual content involving** non-consent, incapacity (a guest or the player who is too drunk to agree), coercion as reward, or anything illegal. When the player is very drunk, intimate options are greyed out with a line like *"Not tonight. You can barely stand."*
- **Debt pressure stays in the story.** The Baron can threaten the tavern, but sex is never a way to pay him off. His menace is political, not sexual.
- **Age gate** on first launch, and a content settings menu (7.6).

### 7.2 Tier 1: Flirting and innuendo (built)

- Flirty dialogue that depends on your Flirt (shy, warm or bold) and your body.
- Smutty gossip when Respect is low. Guests leer at you and whisper about you.
- Lustful guests whose desire comes out in what they say once it passes a threshold.

### 7.3 Tier 2: Strip dares (built, to be expanded)

- A guest who's had a few goes for one of your garments, in wardrobe order (apron, belt, bodice and so on).
- **Accept:** the room cheers, you get tips and Fame, you lose Respect, and Lust in the room goes up.
- **Refuse:** the guest reacts according to their mood, and you keep your dignity.
- **Planned additions:**
  - Garments are lost for the night and can be won back or bought back.
  - **Tavern games:** dice and card games where clothing is the stake.
  - **Dancing on the bar:** a timed rhythm minigame that pays out big when the room's Lust is high.
  - Comic-panel close-ups for big moments, rendered from the real model.

### 7.4 Tier 2b: Guest touches (built)

When she serves a guest, they may reach for her. **Three numbers** decide what happens:

| Number | Belongs to | Range | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Boldness** | the touch | 1–10 | How forward the act is. Brushing fingers is 1, a kiss or a chest grab is 7, the invitation upstairs is 10 |
| **Nerve** | the guest | 0–10 | How bold this guest will be right now. They only reach for touches at or below their nerve |
| **Desire** | the innkeeper | 0–10 | How ready she is. She can only *let* a touch happen once her Desire reaches its boldness − 1 |

**What sets a guest's nerve:** guest type (soldiers 3, smugglers 2, farmers and fugitives 1), plus their lust (×0.6), their mood (Lustful or Drunk +1.5, Scared −2, Gloomy or Idolizing −1), the room's respect for her (low +1.5, high −1.5), +1 for every touch she's already let them have tonight, and −3 if she's slapped them.

**How often:** the chance a guest reaches as she's serving them is 8% plus 5% per point of nerve, up to 55%. The touch is picked at random, weighted toward the boldest their nerve allows, and toward touches not already tried tonight.

**Her Desire:**
- **Desire** (lasts between nights, saved): +0.5 each time she lets a touch happen, +0.8 when she leans in, +0.15 even when she pulls away (curiosity), −0.2 when she slaps.
- **Tonight's heat** (resets at dawn): +1 when she lets a touch happen, +2 when she leans in. It adds a quarter of itself to her Desire for the night.
- **Drink** adds up to +1.2, until she's sloppy drunk.

**Her answers** (the moment freezes as the hand comes in; the player chooses before it lands):

| Answer | When offered | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Let it happen** | Desire ≥ boldness − 1 | Gold = boldness. Respect −⌊boldness/3⌋. Fame +1 from boldness 4. The guest turns Lustful |
| **Lean into it** | Desire ≥ boldness + 1 | Gold = boldness×2 + 1. Respect −1 more. Fame +2, Flirt +1 |
| **Slap them** | Always | Respect +1 (boldness 3+) or +2 (6+). The guest turns Angry and their nerve drops |
| **Pull away** | Always (listed last, so Esc is always safe) | Respect +1 from boldness 5. No change in mood |

**Her thoughts:** each touch has three inner thoughts: *not ready* (Desire below what it needs), *torn* (just enough), and *wanting it* (2 or more over). The first time she lets each kind of touch happen she has a "first time" thought ("My first kiss. In a tavern. With the whole room watching."). After 3 times she's confident and teasing ("That'll cost you extra."). How her voice changes over the whole run (Sheltered, Curious, Willing, Known) is in STORY.md, section 4b.

**Safety rules (in code):** if she or the guest is sloppy drunk, only Slap and Pull away are offered. Boldness-10 invitations are for romance partners only and are never picked in the room. For now only the sister is reached for.

#### Animation

- **Comic panel** (`touchcomic.ts`): a slanted panel like the drink comic, rendering her live from the real model in the dithered look. It frames her face, chest or hips depending on where the touch lands, and turns her back to the camera for the rear touches so she looks back over her shoulder. The guest's hand comes in from the side in their faction's sleeve colour.
  - **Let it happen:** the hand lands, she jolts and ducks her head, and her cheeks blush.
  - **Lean in:** she melts into the hand, head tipped, with a heart and "mmh~".
  - **Slap:** her arm swings out, "SLAP!", and the hand spins away.
  - **Pull away:** she twists out of reach, "eep!", and the hand grabs at air.
  - Rear and chest touches set the soft-part springs bouncing.
- **In the room:** her body jolts, melts or recoils, and the touched soft part bounces on the spring physics.

### 7.5 Respect × lust: how guests talk to you (planned)

Today respect only changes which lines get picked. The plan is to combine the room's respect with each guest's lust into one **tone** that changes mid-conversation as the numbers move:

| | Low lust (0–5) | High lust (6–10) |
|---|---|---|
| **High respect** (4+) | **Admiring:** warm, they confide in you | **Smitten:** they want you but hold back and ask first. The only tone that leads to romance |
| **Middle** | **Casual** | **Teasing:** cheeky, bawdy |
| **Low respect** (−3 or below) | **Contempt:** cold, they tip copper | **Leering:** crude offers, bold touches |
| **Very low** (−7 or below) | **Lost:** you're furniture. They lie when asked, but let secrets slip in front of you | **Lost:** they grope and never ask |

When the tone shifts, the guest says a **shift line** (Teasing → Leering: *"Everyone said you were easy. Didn't believe them till now."*; Leering → Smitten after being put in their place: *"...Sorry. That was the ale talking."*).

**What losing respect does today:** smutty gossip about you (30% of table talk), leering calls instead of orders (50%), crude lines to your face, higher guest lust, +15% strip dares, and with touches, bolder guests. **What it doesn't do yet:** cost money, trust or secrets. Planned costs: low respect stops guests confiding in you and lowers village trust (making *The Village Rises* harder). Respect falls fast and climbs back slowly.

### 7.6 Tier 3: Romance and intimacy (planned)

**Romanceable regulars:** one main romance for each guest faction, all of them adults with their own arc:

Every route works with either sibling, and every route can be romantic, physical, or both.

**How it works:**

1. **Affection** builds per character, through serving them, protecting them, keeping their secrets and private talks.
2. **Private talk** is a booth or a corner table during service. You get to know them and flirt.
3. **Invitation:** once Affection and their Lust are both high, they ask (or you ask) to stay after closing.
4. **Bedtime scene:** a visual-novel sequence in the closed tavern or the upstairs rooms. The player picks the tone (tender, playful, bold) and can stop at any point.
5. **Afterwards:** the morning-after scene, plus what it changes. Their faction's Trust goes up, gossip spreads if anyone saw, and story options open.

**Scene presentation:** pixel-art CG panels in the existing `cg.ts` / comic style. Full nudity is allowed. How explicit it gets follows the content setting.

### 7.7 Tier 4: The upstairs rooms (planned, Act 2)

- **House Plans** unlocks a second floor with rentable rooms.

- Staff are NPCs with consent and wages. Treating them badly (low Kindness, high Greed) leads to them walking out and to a staff-revolt event.

## 8. Characters and factions

### 8.1 Guest factions

| Faction | Usual mood | Wants | Pays in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Farmers** | Gloomy, Happy | Cheap food, someone to complain to | Small coin, village Trust |
| **Soldiers / knights** | Drunk, Angry, Lustful | Ale, a fight, attention | Good coin, lower Heat |
| **Smugglers** | Scheming | Discretion, a back room | Big tips, secrets |
| **Fugitives** | Scared | Hiding, food without questions | Gratitude, rare info |

- **Knights are one faction:** shared colours and badge, helmets off by default.
- **Variety:** guests should never look like copies of one body. Mix ages, builds and types (the nerd, the old man, the brute, the dandy, the heavy-set farmer, the wiry thief and so on).

### 8.2 Story characters

- **The Baron.** The antagonist. Cold and patient. Never seen in the tavern until the final night.
- **The tax collector.** The Baron's ears. He comes at last call.
- **Grandma Wren** (deceased). Seen in letters, a ledger and flashbacks. She knew every secret on the pass.
- **Mother and Father.** Off-screen, and a pull on the story (letters, money sent home, Father's mystery).

## 9. Gossip and secrets

- Every night carries a **rumour** (who's hiding salt, who deserted, who owes whom).
- Rumours come out at party tables when you drink with guests, or are overheard **if you're close enough**. From further away it's only whispering.
- **At last call,** what you heard becomes a choice: tell the truth, lie, sell it, or say nothing. Each choice moves Gold, Trust and Heat.
- **Gossip about you** comes back through the guests. About 2,000 lines, keyed to your reputation, mood pairs and clashing moods. They don't repeat until the rest have been used.
- **Adult tie-in:** lovers talk. A romance partner may let a secret slip in bed, and you choose whether to use it. Using it betrays them.
