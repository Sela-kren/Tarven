Tarven sits under cold mountains. The official chapel on the high street speaks of order, the pass, and the Baron’s peace. The people who drink in your tavern speak of warmer things. Both are true.

## The Small Pantheon

Most folk in Tarven do not worship a single god. They light a candle for one, curse another, and leave a coin or a lock of hair for a third depending on the night.

### 1. Veyra — Goddess of Desire and the Warm Body
**Domain:** Lust, passion, the heat that rises after the first cup, the act itself, lovers who stay past closing.  
**Appearance:** A woman with dark hair, bare shoulders, and a cup that never empties. Sometimes shown with a half-open bodice and a knowing smile. In cheap carvings she is simply a pair of legs and a raised cup.  
**Worship:**  
- Innkeepers leave a drop of the first pour on the hearth for her.  
- Couples who want a child (or just a good night) whisper her name in bed.  
- Strip dares and dancing on the bar are half-jokes and half-prayers.  
**Chapel status:** Tolerated but not official. Brother Oden will not name her from the pulpit. Guests who are very drunk will.  
**Sex aspect:** She *is* the god of sex. Every other god’s bedroom stories eventually mention her.

### 2. Bram — God of Revelry, Wine, and the Night That Won’t End
**Domain:** Drinking, singing, dice, the moment when everyone stops caring about tomorrow, and the sex that follows when the room is loud enough.  
**Appearance:** A fat, laughing man with a cracked mug and a belt full of dice. Often shown with his arm around Veyra.  
**Worship:**  
- First round of the night is sometimes poured “for Bram.”  
- Tavern games (dice, cards, clothing stakes) are said to be his.  
**Sex aspect:** He does not invent desire, but he removes the last reason to say no. High Lust nights in the tavern are called “Bram’s weather.”

### 3. The Mother of Fields (also called Mira or simply “the Mother”)
**Domain:** Harvest, livestock, childbirth, the marriage bed, and the fertility that keeps farms alive through the winter.  
**Appearance:** A broad, strong woman with wheat in her hair and a child on one hip. In private carvings she is shown with her skirts lifted.  
**Worship:**  
- Farmers leave the first sheaf or a cup of milk.  
- Wedding nights are her rites. Some still practice the old “field blessing” (a couple lying together on the edge of the field at planting).  
**Sex aspect:** Fertility is not abstract. The Mother’s blessings are sexual and practical. Low-respect innkeepers are sometimes accused of “stealing the Mother’s heat” when too many couples leave the tavern unsatisfied.

### 4. The Stone-Hearted (or Oathbinder)
**Domain:** Vows, contracts, the Baron’s justice, the strength of the mountains, and the marriage promise that is sealed in bed.  
**Appearance:** A tall figure of stone or a knight with a closed helm. Never shown nude.  
**Worship:** Official chapel god. Brother Oden’s sermons are mostly about him.  
**Sex aspect:** Quiet but present. A marriage is not finished until the couple has lain together under his name. Breaking that vow (adultery, refusal, etc.) is said to crack the mountain. The Lantern (the brothel path) is an open insult to him.

### 5. The Cold One (minor, local)
**Domain:** Winter, the high pass, the dead who freeze on the road, and the sharp desire that comes when the body is cold and the fire is close.  
**Appearance:** A pale figure in a thin cloak, sometimes with frost on the skin and heat in the eyes.  
**Worship:** Almost none in the chapel. Travelers and smugglers leave a sip of spirits outside on the coldest nights.  
**Sex aspect:** The contrast. Desire that only appears when everything else is freezing. Some of the darker, more desperate bedroom stories in Tarven are blamed on her.

## The Church of Veyra (Future Content — Sex-Focused Cult)

Veyra’s full worship is not the quiet candles of the high-street chapel. In places where people are sexually frustrated, a different kind of church grows: the Church of Veyra (sometimes called the Warm House or the Open Temple).

**Core teaching**  
Veyra’s stated goal is simple: relieve human sexual stress. Frustration with a partner, long dry spells, mismatched desire, or just the everyday pressure of wanting and not getting — all of it is a kind of tightness in the body. The church’s job is to loosen it through talk, ritual, and a lot of sex play.  

People who come are mostly those who are sexually frustrated with their partners or sexually frustrated in general. The church does not judge why someone is frustrated. It only asks whether they want the tightness eased.

### Clergy Ranks (Isekai-style Hierarchy)

The Church of Veyra has a full internal rank system for its priests and staff, similar to the detailed church hierarchies found in isekai stories. Visitors stay outside this ladder. Once someone commits to serving inside the church, they climb these ranks. Higher ranks require the slut outfit and permanent internal toys.

**Key rules for ranks 3 and above**
- Both men and women wear the slut-style clothing once they reach Priest rank.

### Visitor Side (unchanged in spirit)
People who only come for relief are still just Visitors. They do not wear the outfits or toys unless they later choose to join the clergy. The ranks above are only for those who live and work inside the church.

### What Happens in the Church

- **Talking sessions**: A Priest or High Priest sits with the visitor. The visitor explains the frustration (partner won’t touch them, partner is cold, they want more than their partner gives, they have no partner, etc.). The listener’s job is to understand the exact shape of the stress.
- **Relief rites**: Once the talking is done, the church engages in sex play aimed at releasing that stress. It can be simple (hands, mouths, toys) or more elaborate depending on what the visitor asks for and what the priests are willing to give. Multiple people can participate.
- **Ongoing care**: Some visitors return regularly. Higher-ranking priests may be assigned to specific frustrated people for a period of time.
- **Public or semi-public elements**: Because the teaching is that hiding the desire makes the stress worse, some rites happen where others can see. This is optional for visitors; it is expected for ranks 3 and above.

### Relationship to the Rest of Tarven

- The official high-street chapel (Brother Oden, the Stone-Hearted) does not approve of this church. They call it excess. The Baron’s people treat it as a potential source of scandal or a useful outlet, depending on the night.
- In the tavern, people speak of the Church of Veyra in the same tone they speak of the Lantern: loud, useful, and not quite respectable.
